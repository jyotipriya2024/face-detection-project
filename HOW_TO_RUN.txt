HOW TO RUN FACE DETECTION
==========================

STEP 1 - Open terminal in VS Code
   Press Ctrl + ` (backtick key)

STEP 2 - Run these two commands:

   cd "d:\Users\dibya\jyoti\face-detection-project"

   & "C:\Program Files\Python311\python.exe" detect_face.py

   A window will open showing your webcam with green boxes on detected faces.

STEP 3 - To stop
   Press Q in the camera window, or close it.


------------------------------------------------------------
TO TEST ON A PHOTO (instead of webcam):

   & "C:\Program Files\Python311\python.exe" detect_face.py "C:\path\to\photo.jpg"

   Replace C:\path\to\photo.jpg with the actual path to your image.
